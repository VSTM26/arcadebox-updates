ShowerTime Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

ShowerTime ("the app") is a voice clock that announces the time out loud at intervals or at specific times you choose.

Data collected: none. ShowerTime does not collect, store on any server, sell, or share any personal data.

  • The app has no accounts, no sign-ups, and no analytics.
  • The app contains no advertising and no third-party tracking SDKs.
  • Your settings (announcement interval, chosen times, voice phrase, auto-stop) are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage, and are deleted when you uninstall the app.
  • Time announcements are spoken on your device using Android's built-in Text-to-Speech. ShowerTime does not record audio and does not send your voice, your settings, or any other information anywhere.
  • The app does not access the internet.

Permissions the app uses, and why:

  • Foreground service (media playback): keeps your announcement session running while the app is in the background or the screen is locked, after you tap GO. It stops completely when you tap STOP.
  • Notifications: shows the running session with a STOP button.
  • Wake lock: prevents the phone from sleeping in the middle of an announcement session you started.
  • Vibrate: system feedback only.

Children: The app is a general-audience utility and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes: If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
